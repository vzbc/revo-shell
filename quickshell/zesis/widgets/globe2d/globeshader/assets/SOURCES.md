Earth textures, from https://github.com/turban/webgl-earth (companion repo to
"Creating a WebGL Earth with three.js" by Bjørn Sandvik, blog.mastermaps.com).

- `earth_elevation.png` (from `elev_bump_4k.jpg`) — elevation/bump map. Blog post
  cites shadedrelief.com's Tom Patterson "Natural Earth III" collection (public
  domain Natural Earth data) as the source.
- `earth_watermask.png` (from `water_4k.png`) — binary land/water mask, same
  texture bundle. Origin not separately documented by the author, inferred to
  be from the same texture-set lineage (used as a specular/water mask in the
  original three.js demo).
